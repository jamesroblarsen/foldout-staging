          How to play

          Each day there's a new city, shown as an unlabelled 3D model: a small piece of it, chosen to show how the city was planned or how it grew. Drag with two fingers (or the right mouse button) to tilt and turn it.

          You have six guesses. After each wrong guess the map gives a little more away:

          1. Streets and buildings, 800 m across
          2. Water and parks appear
          3. Zoom out to 1.2 km, railways appear
          4. 1.6 km, plus a hint about the city's pattern
          5. The full 2 km sheet, plus the continent
          6. The country

          Each guess shows how far away and in which direction the answer lies.

          🟩 right🟨 under 500 km🟧 under 2,000 km🟥 further

          Look for grids and their angles, ring roads where walls stood, medieval tangles, canals, diagonals cutting across blocks, block sizes against the scale bar.
